Seminar Rules

  • Groups: two to three students each.
  • Grading:
    • 80% seminar thesis
    • 20% presentation, in which every group member speaks.
  • Seminar thesis:
    • Length: 8–12 pages main text, including figures and tables; title page, table of contents, references, and appendix do not count.
    • Format: PDF with 12 pt font, 1.5 line spacing, and 2.5 cm margins. The thesis templates (Chapter 2) already meet these requirements.
    • Code: Only short snippets in the main text. Submit the full code separately so that all results can be reproduced (fixed random seed).
    • Submission: thesis.pdf and a ZIP file of your complete thesis folder (text, code, and literature). Deadline: TBA.
  • Exam registration (via campus.KU) deadline: January 21.
